2. Information used by the apps

The apps do not automatically collect or transmit personal data to developer-operated servers. The current apps have no account registration, developer backend, advertising, or analytics SDKs.

The apps do not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising identifiers, or health data such as heart rate and step count.

The watch face uses system-provided time, date, time zone, 12/24-hour format, device language, appearance settings, and active/ambient display mode to render the dial. This information is used on the device and is not sent to the developer.

3. Local storage

The companion app stores your language preference locally. On supported Android versions, app language is also managed by the system app-language settings. Watch face color choices are managed by Wear OS.

These settings preserve your preferences and are not synchronized to developer-operated servers. You can change them in the app or watch face editor. Companion preferences can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app; watch face preferences can be managed through the watch system.

4. Google Play and system services

The Google Play button opens Google Play or an external browser. Google handles store accounts, purchases, payments, downloads, and related service data under its own policies. These apps do not access card numbers or directly process payments.

Android, Wear OS, device manufacturers, browsers, and Google services may process information under their own settings and policies, separately from these apps. Please read Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Information you send to support

If you email the developer, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as your message, device model, app version, screenshots, or an order reference. We use this information to respond, resolve issues, and handle privacy requests.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive information. Support information is not used for marketing or sold. Email service providers may process messages to provide email services.

6. Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising or analytics. The apps do not automatically transmit personal data for the developer to share.

Support messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations. Information may be disclosed when required by law or a valid legal request. Email the developer to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent; requests will be handled according to applicable requirements.

There are no app accounts to delete. Deletion of data held by Google Play or other third-party services is handled by those services and is not controlled by this app developer.

7. Security

Local preferences use app storage and protections provided by Android/Wear OS. Access to support messages is limited to handling requests and related obligations. No electronic storage or communication method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

The apps have no registration, advertising, tracking, or automatic personal data collection features, including for children. If a child’s personal information is sent through support and you wish to request its deletion, contact the developer.
